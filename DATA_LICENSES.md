# Data sources and licences

The map files in `public/data/` are generated by `scripts/build-maps.mjs` from:

## Natural Earth (public domain)
World countries (India point-of-view edition), rivers and physical regions.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ - "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website are in the public domain."

## INDIAN-SHAPEFILES by datta07 (MIT)
India state and union territory boundaries. https://github.com/datta07/INDIAN-SHAPEFILES
(Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu are merged here into the single union territory created in 2020.)

## Boundaries
Both sources show India's borders as officially depicted by the Government of India
(all of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh). The world map is the Natural Earth "India" point of view.
These are simplified for a children's app and are not survey-accurate.
